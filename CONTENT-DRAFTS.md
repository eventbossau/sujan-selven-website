## About

### Hero lead
Sujan Selven is a community worker and human rights advocate who has lived in Western Sydney for more than 20 years. He cares about Cumberland because the people here deserve to be heard — and to thrive, not just get by.

### Opening paragraph
Sujan’s story is rooted in community. After more than two decades living in Western Sydney, he has seen how housing, energy bills, local services and neighbourhood care shape whether families can stay, grow and feel secure.

### Background
Sujan has made Western Sydney home for more than 20 years. That long connection to place — and to the people who live here — sits at the centre of his public work.

### Experience
As a community worker and human rights advocate, Sujan has worked closely with local communities for many years. That experience gives him a practical understanding of the pressures people face, and a focus on clear, human outcomes over jargon.

### In the community — headline
Working with people, where they are

### In the community — body
Sujan’s approach is grounded in listening first: understanding what residents are dealing with in their street, school, home and workplace. The issues change, but the reason stays the same — people over politics.

That local work connects directly to the priorities on this site: affordable housing, clean energy, cost of living, and community and care across Cumberland.

### Greens affiliation line
Sujan is committed to The Greens’ vision for real change — ecological sustainability, grassroots democracy, social justice, and peace and non-violence — applied to the everyday realities of Western Sydney.
